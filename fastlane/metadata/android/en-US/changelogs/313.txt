Version 0.10.0

- Optional PIN lock (4 to 8 digits), with biometrics still available as an alternative
- Shared 2FAuth accounts are now shown as read-only and marked as shared, and their codes are fetched from the server
- Secure-storage failures now recover on retry instead of leaving the app stuck on an unlock screen
- Updated Flutter SDK and application dependencies
- Android build configuration updated for current Flutter and Gradle compatibility
